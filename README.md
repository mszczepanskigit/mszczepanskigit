## About Me

I'm a **Test Automation Engineer** working on embedded systems, CI/CD, and device-level testing. I like building automation that is useful in practice, not just impressive on paper: tests that help teams ship with confidence, catch regressions early, and make debugging easier.

My background combines **embedded Linux**, **Python**, **networking**, and **test automation**, with experience across Smart TVs, set-top boxes, routers, and cable modems. I tend to work close to the product and care about the full picture: test strategy, implementation, reporting, documentation, and handover.

I also have a strong analytical background from **Applied Mathematics** at the University of Wrocław, where I completed both my bachelor's and master's studies.
